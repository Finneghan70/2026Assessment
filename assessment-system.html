    BC proficiency scale — Grades 4 & 5

    E
    Emerging
    Beginning to demonstrate knowledge and skills relevant to the expected learning. May be at initial stages of grade-level expectations, or working toward them.
    D
    Developing
    Demonstrating a partial understanding of the concepts and competencies relevant to the expected learning. Progressing toward expectations. Not the same as failing.
    P
    Proficient
    Demonstrating a complete understanding of the concepts and competencies relevant to the expected learning. This is the target for all students.
    X
    Extending
    Demonstrating a sophisticated understanding that goes beyond the expected learning. Applying skills in novel and complex ways across contexts.
    Important for parent conferences: Proficient is the goal for every student — not Extending. "Developing at the beginning of the year is expected and healthy. All students move through the scale at different rates. The scale describes current performance, not a fixed label.

    How to use the scale in conferences

    Student-led conference
    Students explain where they are on the scale for each area using their portfolio evidence. Teacher facilitates. Parent listens and asks questions. Student sets next goals.
    Teacher–parent conference
    Use the conference prep view to generate a profile summary. Walk through the scale for each area. Share specific evidence from the portfolio. Agree on 2–3 next steps together.
    Informal check-in
    Quick 1:1 during CAFE conferring or Math Daily 3 small group. Update the student's profile immediately after. These micro-assessments are the richest data in the system.
    FSA context (Grade 4)
    The Foundation Skills Assessment uses the same Emerging–Extending scale. Results do not appear on report cards but can confirm or prompt revision of your proficiency judgements.
